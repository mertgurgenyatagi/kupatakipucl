# Special Lobby — Round 7 answers

### 1. Chat and Rankings both get a lobby toggle now (Forum was dropped). Does the Predictions page — where picks actually get submitted — get one too, or does it stay global-only?

**Answer:** Stays global-only — predictions are one shared thing, not per-lobby

### 2. When you're viewing a lobby's chat, should “who's online” reflect just that lobby's members, or true site-wide online status regardless of which view you're in?

**Answer:** Scoped — only show lobby members' online status while inside that lobby's view

### 3. Chat already has a search feature. Once lobbies exist, does searching stay confined to whichever view you're currently on, or search across General and all your lobbies at once?

**Answer:** Confined to the current view — search General, or search one lobby, never mixed

### 4. Is there any appetite for a “within just this lobby” filtered version of existing widgets (like who's-online or who-hasn't-submitted lists), or is that explicitly out of scope, same spirit as skipping stats?

**Answer:** Actually, yes — worth scoping in now

### 5. The switcher arrow's active/hover color — reuse the site's existing accent color like every other interactive element, or give this feature a small distinct visual identity of its own?

**Answer:** Reuse the existing site accent — consistency over novelty

### 6. Next to the cycle-arrow, should the current view's name always be visible (“General ›”, “Fener Grubu ›”), or just the bare arrow with no label?

**Answer:** Always show the name — context should be visible at a glance

### 7. That small “creator” tag in the member list (locked in Round 6) — plain text label, or a small icon?

**Answer:** A small icon (crown, star) instead of text

### 8. System messages (join/leave/removal/rename/created) in the chat feed — should they look visually distinct from real human messages, or blend in as if “System” were just another sender?

**Answer:** Visually distinct — centered, muted, no bubble, clearly not a person talking

### 9. The “you're at your lobby limit” and “this invite is no longer valid” messages — do these show as a small inline page/state, or as a toast/snackbar that pops up and fades?

**Answer:** Toast/snackbar — a small transient notice, page underneath stays put

### 10. The very first time the switcher arrow appears for someone (right after their first lobby ever) — any kind of one-time highlight/tour, or zero onboarding, consistent with Round 5's “they'll figure it out”?

**Answer:** Zero onboarding — same as everywhere else, no tour needed
