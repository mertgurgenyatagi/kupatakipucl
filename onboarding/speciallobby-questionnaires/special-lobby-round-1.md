# Special Lobby — Round 1 answers

_Generated 2026-07-29_

### 1. Who can create a special lobby?

**Answer:** Any signed-in participant, but capped at a small number of lobbies per person

### 2. How do people actually get into a lobby you created?

**Answer:** You share an invite link/code — anyone with it can join instantly

### 3. Can someone belong to more than one special lobby at the same time?

**Answer:** Yes, but capped at a small number (e.g. 3)

### 4. Should lobbies be discoverable, or fully invite-only and invisible to outsiders?

**Answer:** Fully private — invite-only, no public listing anywhere

### 5. What should the lobby's “rankings” tab actually show?

**Answer:** The exact same leaderboard math as the site-wide rankings, just filtered to lobby members

### 6. Who can rename the lobby or change its image after it's created?

**Answer:** Any member at all

### 7. Is the lobby chat a totally separate thread from the site-wide chat, or related to it?

**Answer:** Your call.

### 8. Same question, but for the forum — separate, filtered, or skipped entirely?

**Answer:** Actually, skip forum for lobbies after all — just chat + rankings

### 9. Can a member leave on their own, and can the creator remove someone?

**Answer:** Both — members can leave anytime, creator can remove anyone

### 10. What happens to a lobby if its creator deletes their account or leaves it?

**Answer:** Ownership auto-transfers to the next-oldest member — the lobby survives
