# Special Lobby — Round 3 answers

_Generated 2026-07-29_

### 1. Lobby name — what's the character cap? (Profiles are capped at 15, forum posts at 360, for reference.)

**Answer:** 15 chars

### 2. Does a lobby's name need to be unique, or can two lobbies share a name?

**Answer:** Doesn't matter — lobbies are private, duplicates are harmless

### 3. Within its 1-day window, can the same invite link admit multiple different people, or is it single-use?

**Answer:** Reusable — anyone who has the link within the window can join

### 4. Someone clicks an invite link that's expired, or that points at a lobby that's since been deleted. What do they see?

**Answer:** A clear “this invite is no longer valid” message

### 5. Does an invite link only work for people already signed up on the site, or could it also work for someone brand new?

**Answer:** Already-registered participants only — this is closed to the ~150 people on the site

### 6. The tab-switcher for picking a lobby — is it one shared control (pick a lobby once, applies to chat and rankings both), or independent per page?

**Answer:** Once the user is in a lobby, on every "page" or widget or whatever, the default is special lobby, not general. They need to switch to general and they do this independently for each ocurrence.

### 7. Should a lobby's chat tab show an unread/activity indicator when something new happened there?

**Answer:** Yes — same idea as anywhere else unread activity gets flagged

### 8. Where does “create a new lobby” actually live?

**Answer:** Inside that same chat/rankings tab-switcher panel — a “+ New Lobby” entry alongside your existing ones

### 9. If you get removed from a lobby while you weren't online, what do you see when you're back?

**Answer:** Nothing — the tab for that lobby just silently disappears next time you check

### 10. Since there's no dedicated lobby page, where do you actually see who's in a lobby?

**Answer:** Inside that same management panel/modal — a simple member list lives there
