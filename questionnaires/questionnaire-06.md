# Great Leap — Round 6 answers

## One combined score, or two separate ones?

**Q: Once the bracket exists, does a person's overall rank/points combine their league-phase score and their bracket score into one number — the same one shown on the main Leaderboard, the mini-leaderboard widget, and the rank-history graph — or do the two stay as genuinely separate scores/leaderboards?**

Yep, it combines.

**Q: If they combine into one number — does the rank-history graph's line just keep tracking that same combined rank straight through the bracket rounds (so a big bracket week can swing someone's line hard), or does something visually mark the handoff from "league scoring" to "league + bracket scoring" on the graph?**

There is a visual mark, but not massive.

## People who didn't finish something

**Q: Is it possible for someone to have a profile but never actually submit a league prediction (onboarded, then missed the deadline)? If so, what do they see in the mini-leaderboard / rank graph / their own home once it's started — no rank at all, a rank of last place, something else?**

Don't think too hard about this. I mean, implement something so no bugs occur but I assure you this will not happen.

**Q: Is submitting a bracket prediction open to anyone logged in at that point regardless of whether they did a league prediction, or does someone need a league prediction on file to even access the bracket door?**

They can do a bracket prediction without a league one.

## Signed-out, during the bracket rounds

**Q: You said signed-out home is "almost a copy of the logged-in league leaderboard page" once it's started — does that stay true unchanged all the way through the gap and the knockout rounds too, or does a signed-out visitor ever get to see any bracket-related view (even a public, nobody's-personal-picks version), or is the bracket strictly a logged-in-only thing end to end?**

They can see the bracket too.
